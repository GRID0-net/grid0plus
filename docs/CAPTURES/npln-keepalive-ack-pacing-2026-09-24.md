# PresenceService KeepAlive acknowledgment pacing

The two working Nextendo hall captures contain 1,871 requests/1,873 replies and 2,911 requests/2,914 replies on `PresenceService/KeepAlive`. The small excess is explained by opening/idle heartbeats. Over their captured lifetimes, this is approximately 20 replies per second. These counts and timing are protocol observations only; no captured messages or credentials are reproduced here.

A SwitchNet-side console capture on 2026-09-24 instead measured 101 ping/reply round trips in two seconds (~50/s) immediately before the hall communication error. SwitchNet responded by dropping pings received within a one-second throttle. The 14:11:45 hardware attempt after that change returned only 15 response bytes on KeepAlive (the opening heartbeat) before the console canceled its streams at 14:12:05. The client sends its next ping after receiving the previous reply, so dropping one ping's reply leaves it waiting. The independent 20-second ticker was too late to recover the stream.

SwitchNet now paces *each* reply at least 50ms after the previous one, rather than discarding any reply. That bounds the feedback loop near the rate seen in both working captures while preserving the request/reply exchange. A gRPC test sends pings immediately after receiving responses and checks that each is acknowledged before the stream's deadline. Whether this also clears the hall error still requires a hardware retry.
